# Client original photo replacement review — 24 October 2026

All 341 supplied JPGs have a unique matching photograph in the existing 2026
catalogue. Matching used the existing photo composition and image comparison;
the 42 discrepancies below were also reviewed side by side. The 61 products
from the 2027 PDF, factory photos, project photos and Signature renders are
outside this replacement set.

The replacements preserve each source's composition and colour, with sRGB
conversion where needed and quality-92 WebP encoding. Responsive sizes never
exceed the source resolution: 299 originals are 1090–1110px wide, six are
787–788px wide, and 36 portrait originals are 346–347px wide. Larger original
files would be needed to improve those small portrait photos further.
